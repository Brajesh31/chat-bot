# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The ChatBuddy project aims to provide a respectful, useful, and welcoming environment for people with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Participation in project spaces is conditional on treating others with dignity and contributing constructively.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions where enabled, project-related communication, and any space where someone is acting on behalf of the project.

## 🌱 Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:
- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Focus criticism on code, decisions, and ideas rather than attacking people.
- Explain technical feedback constructively.
- Welcome questions and beginner contributions.
- Respect different viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Accept good-faith correction and revise inaccurate statements.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing someone's personal information.
- Use inclusive, professional language.
- Follow repository security and contribution guidance.
- Help keep project spaces focused and accessible.

## 🚫 Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:
- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning comments targeting a protected or personal characteristic.
- Threats, intimidation, stalking, or deliberate humiliation.
- Doxxing or publishing private personal information without consent.
- Repeated unwanted contact or sexualized attention without consent.
- Insults, slurs, or sustained hostile behavior.
- Retaliation against someone for making a good-faith report or participating in an investigation.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith interference with project work.
- Publishing private conversations, credentials, access tokens, or other sensitive material.
- Misrepresenting another person's contributions or claiming work without a reasonable basis.

## 🧭 Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:
- Reviewing reports in a timely manner when reasonably possible.
- Applying this policy consistently and proportionately.
- Limiting access to report details to people who need them.
- Avoiding retaliation and discouraging retaliatory behavior.
- Communicating decisions and next steps where it is safe to do so.
- Removing or restricting content that creates an immediate safety or privacy risk.

Maintainers are also expected to follow the same behavior standards as every other participant.

## 📣 Reporting a conduct concern

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately using the contact method on the [@brajesh31 GitHub profile](https://github.com/brajesh31). Include:
- The relevant repository space or communication channel.
- Approximate date and context.
- A description of the behavior and its impact.
- Links or screenshots only where sharing them is appropriate.
- Any requested confidentiality or safety considerations.

Please do not include passwords, access tokens, private user transcripts, or unrelated personal information. If the concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting tools or another appropriate platform-level channel.

Suspected security vulnerabilities must be reported separately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a public conduct issue.

## 🔎 Review and enforcement process

Depending on the report and risk, maintainers may:
1. Acknowledge receipt when reasonably possible.
2. Assess immediate safety, privacy, and retaliation risks.
3. Review relevant context and request clarification when necessary.
4. Give the reported participant a reasonable opportunity to respond when appropriate.
5. Determine whether the policy was violated.
6. Apply a proportionate corrective action.
7. Communicate the outcome to involved parties to the extent privacy permits.

Reports will be handled discreetly, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed if investigation or platform enforcement requires disclosure.

## ⚖️ Possible consequences

Depending on severity, repetition, and context, actions may include:
- A private or public warning.
- Request to edit or remove content.
- Temporary limits on participation.
- Removal of comments, issue content, or pull-request content.
- Temporary suspension from project spaces.
- Permanent exclusion from project spaces.
- Referral to GitHub or another platform for policy enforcement.

Maintainers may take immediate action when needed to reduce a serious safety, privacy, or security risk.

## 🧩 Appeals and conflicts

A participant may request reconsideration by privately explaining why the decision appears inconsistent or based on incomplete information. An appeal should not include retaliation or repeated unwanted contact. If the original decision-maker is directly involved in the report, platform reporting or an alternative reviewer should be used where available.

## 🛡️ No retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith, provides relevant evidence, or participates in a review is itself unacceptable conduct.

## 📚 Project boundaries

The project is a small educational frontend repository. Discussions should remain respectful even when a requested feature is out of scope or technically unsuitable. Disagreement about architecture, limitations, licenses, or security must not become personal hostility.
