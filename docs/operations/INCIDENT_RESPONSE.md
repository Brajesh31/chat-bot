# 🚨 Incident Response

Critical: credential exposure or malicious release. High: exploitable defect or sensitive-data exposure. Medium: major issue with workaround. Low: cosmetic/limited issue.

1. Record time, commit, deployment, and symptoms.
2. Contain and revoke exposed credentials.
3. Preserve sanitized evidence.
4. Determine affected assets and flows.
5. Verify and deploy a fix.
6. Confirm recovery.
7. Write a postmortem with owners and actions.

Report vulnerabilities privately through SECURITY.md.
